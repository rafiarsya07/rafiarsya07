About me

I'm a Software Engineering student at FSKTM, Universiti Malaya, originally from Indonesia. I like building things end to end: designing the database, writing the backend, and getting the frontend to feel right, then self-hosting the result on my own mini PC instead of handing it off to a platform.

Outside class I lead the Website Division at PPI Malaysia and head the Art Exhibition for IDFEST at UM.

What I'm building
Nalar — a self-hosted personal blog CMS built from scratch. Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL on the backend, a single-file vanilla JS reading experience on the front, in-browser reading recommendations with no server-side profile, scheduled publishing, and a full admin dashboard.
CampusBay — a student-powered marketplace for digital "Drops" (notes, guides, templates) and physical campus listings. Next.js on Cloudflare Workers, D1, and R2, built as a real production MVP rather than a class project.
IDFEST 2026 — the festival site for PPI-UM's cultural event, built with a two-person team in Next.js and Tailwind from Figma mockups I helped turn into a working, animated site.
hire.rafiarsya.com and templates.rafiarsya.com — a freelance web development service and a storefront of ready-made site templates, both static and deployed on Cloudflare Pages.
